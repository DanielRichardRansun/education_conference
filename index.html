About the Summit

What is GECW 2026 International Conference?

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly reshaping the global education ecosystem. The Global Educator Conference & Workshop 2026 (GECW 2026), themed “AI in the Future of Learning: Friend or Foe?”, seeks to strengthen cross-sector collaboration to generate practical classroom solutions, ethical frameworks, and measurable impact for educators worldwide.

Focus Area & Guiding Questions

Focus Area Guiding Questions
1. AI Lightening Your Load How have you used AI to reduce workload, automate routine tasks, or enhance teaching without replacing your professional judgment? What practical tools or workflows have you tested?
2. The Dark Side How have you addressed cheating, data privacy, or loss of human connection in an AI-integrated classroom? What strategies, policies, or interventions did you develop and test?
3. Preparing AI Ready School How are you transforming your school's culture, curriculum, policies, or professional development to become AI ready? What systemic changes have you piloted? How do you prepare leadership, teachers, students, and parents collectively for an AI-integrated environment?
