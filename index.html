About GECW 2026

3 Days. 3 Perspectives.
One shared question.

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly reshaping the global education ecosystem. The Global Educator Conference & Workshop 2026 (GECW 2026), themed “AI in the Future of Learning: Friend or Foe?”, seeks to strengthen cross-sector collaboration to generate practical classroom solutions, ethical frameworks, and measurable impact for educators worldwide.

Call for Paper

Focus Area & Guiding Questions

Focus Area 1

AI Lightening Your Load

Exploring how AI can reduce workload and enhance teaching without replacing professional judgment.

How have you used AI to reduce workload, automate routine tasks, or enhance teaching without replacing your professional judgment? What practical tools or workflows have you tested?

Focus Area 2

The Dark Side

Addressing cheating, data privacy, and loss of human connection in an AI-integrated classroom.

How have you addressed cheating, data privacy, or loss of human connection in an AI-integrated classroom? What strategies, policies, or interventions did you develop and test?

Focus Area 3

Preparing AI Ready School

Transforming school culture, curriculum, policies, and professional development to become AI ready.

How are you transforming your school's culture, curriculum, policies, or professional development to become AI ready? What systemic changes have you piloted? How do you prepare leadership, teachers, students, and parents collectively for an AI-integrated environment?
