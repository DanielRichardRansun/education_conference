About GECW 2026

3 Days. 3 Perspectives.
One shared question.

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly reshaping the global education ecosystem. The Global Educator Conference & Workshop 2026 (GECW 2026), themed “AI in the Future of Learning: Friend or Foe?”, seeks to strengthen cross-sector collaboration to generate practical classroom solutions, ethical frameworks, and measurable impact for educators worldwide.

CHOOSE YOUR JOURNEY

Discover the two core tracks of GECW 2026: Academic Conference and Hands-on Workshop

Academic & Research Track
Academic Research & FGD

Conference (Call for Paper)

An immersive 3-day program where educators, researchers, and academic leaders present papers and exchange perspectives in dynamic Focus Group Discussions (FGD) with opportunities for indexed international publication.

Participation Pathways:

Conference Presenter (FGD): Submit paper/abstract, present in FGD sub-themes, and access indexed journal publications (SINTA / International Journal).
Conference Participant: Join all keynote sessions, participate in plenary & FGD discussions, network with global leaders, and receive an accredited certificate.
Explore FGD & Call for Paper
Hands-on & Innovation Track
Ideation, Prototyping & Exhibition

Workshop/Bootcamp

A 3-day immersive hands-on collaborative program where educators translate AI concepts into classroom practice through structured group ideation, rapid prototyping, and a public exhibition.

Learning Pathways:

Workshop Participant: Collaborate in sprint groups, create AI classroom prototypes, learn practical prompt engineering, and take home ready-to-use lesson frameworks.
Showcase innovative classroom tools or lesson models, lead peer ideation, and display prototypes at the dedicated public exhibition.

Focus Area & Guiding Questions

Focus Area 1

AI Lightening Your Load

Exploring how AI can reduce workload and enhance teaching without replacing professional judgment.

How have you used AI to reduce workload, automate routine tasks, or enhance teaching without replacing your professional judgment? What practical tools or workflows have you tested?

Focus Area 2

The Dark Side

Addressing cheating, data privacy, and loss of human connection in an AI-integrated classroom.

How have you addressed cheating, data privacy, or loss of human connection in an AI-integrated classroom? What strategies, policies, or interventions did you develop and test?

Focus Area 3

Preparing AI Ready School

Transforming school culture, curriculum, policies, and professional development to become AI ready.

How are you transforming your school's culture, curriculum, policies, or professional development to become AI ready? What systemic changes have you piloted? How do you prepare leadership, teachers, students, and parents collectively for an AI-integrated environment?
